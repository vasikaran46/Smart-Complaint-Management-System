About SCMS

Building transparent communication between citizens and administration through technology.

Our Mission

The Smart Complaint Management System was created to bridge the gap between citizens and public administration. We believe every concern — big or small — deserves attention, accountability, and a timely resolution.

Our platform provides a structured, digital-first approach to complaint management, replacing inefficient paper-based systems with real-time tracking, automated workflows, and data-driven insights.

Our Vision

We envision a world where civic engagement is effortless and administration is transparent. SCMS empowers citizens to voice their concerns without barriers, while giving departments the tools to respond effectively.

By combining intuitive design with powerful complaint management, we aim to improve public services and build trust between communities and their representatives.

Core Values

The principles that guide everything we build.

Transparency

Every complaint, status update, and resolution is tracked and visible. No hidden processes or unexplained delays.

Accessibility

Designed for everyone — from tech-savvy users to first-time digital users. Simple, clean, and responsive.

Accountability

Clear ownership of every complaint. Departments and staff are assigned, tracked, and measurable.
